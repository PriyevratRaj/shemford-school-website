About Our School

A future-focused CBSE school in the heart of Muzaffarpur

Shemford Futuristic School is a CBSE affiliated institution located at Dumri, Patna Bypass Road, Dumri – Gobarsahi Rd, Muzaffarpur, Bihar 843113.

The school brings together a structured academic curriculum with a strong emphasis on holistic development. Our approach combines classroom learning with co-curricular engagement so that students grow academically, socially and personally.

Campus & Curriculum

Facilities & programs

Academic, co-curricular and developmental programs offered at the school.

View all facilities

Annual Day Function

A vibrant pedagogical tool and celebration of the academic calendar that contributes significantly to students’ cognitive development and skill enhancement. Every student is given a platform to perform and showcase their talent in dancing, singing and acting.

Science Exhibition

Conducted in our school to encourage students to be inquisitive and think analytically. It ignites curiosity and offers chances for students to engage in inquiry-based learning, allowing them to explore hypotheses and find answers on their own.

Investiture Ceremony

A platform to recognise and inculcate leadership qualities among students. By honouring students with leadership positions, the school reinforces the importance of leadership and develops essential leadership skills and responsibility.

Educational Trips

Give students a break from the traditional classroom setting and allow them to learn in a more fun and interactive way — Wildlife Sanctuary, Zoo, Trekking, Excursion, and visits to historical places, monuments and sight-seeing destinations.

Sports Day

Sports are vital for holistic development of students, fostering physical fitness, teamwork, discipline and character. We take sports seriously and organise Sports Day games every year as a significant part of the curriculum.

Science Olympiad Foundation

Promotes students’ scientific reasoning and logical abilities, evaluates understanding of cyber concepts and mathematical abilities, assesses English language skills, and cultivates interest in general knowledge and current events through participation in Science Olympiad Foundation programs for different subjects.

Co-curricular Activities

Aim to fuel learning and build important life skills — leadership, organisation, confidence and socialisation. Math Club, Writing Club, Chess Club, Sports Club, Science Club, Debate Club, Quiz Club and Student Council.

Teacher’s Training Programme

In-house training programmes and workshops provided to teachers as needed, offering opportunities for continuous professional development — new ways, methods, strategies, skills and tools — so they feel confident, happy and motivated to achieve greater things with students.

Vedic Maths & Abacus

A systematic process of calculation that helps eradicate the phobia for Maths among children. With practice, the child is able to do calculations mentally and at great speed, which is helpful in competitive exams. Part of our curriculum since inception.

Coding

Introductory coding classes incorporated as part of the K–12 curriculum. The skills learnt through coding extend beyond the computer and digital use, and are applied to activities that span well beyond the coding space.
